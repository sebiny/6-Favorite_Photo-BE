# 📑 Project Overview

> <mark>**Favorite Photo**</mark> is a platform where users can use points as currency to <mark>**purchase**</mark> photos they love or exchange photo cards with other users.
> Users can also enjoy a fun experience through the <mark>**Surprise Points**</mark> event, which appears once every hour!
> Exchange photo cards with other users, show off your personal collection, and enjoy interacting with other photo card enthusiasts on <mark>**Favorite Photo**</mark>!

# Team Members' Implemented Features

<details>
<summary>
Lee Taebin (Team Lead)

</summary>
<div markdown="1">

* Header Layout & Profile Modal UI
* Photo Card Purchase

  * **Detail View**

    * If the photo card does not belong to the current user, they can purchase it using points or propose an exchange.
  * **Purchase**

    * Users can purchase available photo cards listed in the marketplace using points. When a card is purchased, the buyer's points are deducted by the card price and the seller's points are increased accordingly.
* Photo Card Exchange

  * **Request**

    * Users can propose an exchange for available photo cards listed in the marketplace by selecting one of their own cards to offer.
  * **Cancel**

    * Users can cancel an exchange request they have submitted for a photo card.

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>
Kim Jaewook (Co-Team Lead)

</summary>
<div markdown="1">

* Notifications

  * Notifications display when each event occurred using the following format:

    * **1–23 hours ago**

      * Example: 1 hour ago, 2 hours ago, 3 hours ago, ... 23 hours ago
    * **24 hours–6 days ago**

      * Example: 1 day ago, 2 days ago, 3 days ago, ... 6 days ago
    * **7 days–3 weeks ago**

      * Example: 1 week ago, 2 weeks ago, 3 weeks ago
    * **4 weeks–11 months ago**

      * Example: 1 month ago, 2 months ago, 3 months ago, ... 11 months ago
    * **12 months or more ago**

      * Example: 1 year ago, 2 years ago, 3 years ago, ...
    * **Successful / Failed Exchange Notification**

      * Notifies users when their exchange proposal has been accepted or rejected.
    * **Exchange Proposal Notification**

      * Notifies sellers when another user submits an exchange proposal for their listed photo card.
    * **Purchase Completion Notification**

      * Notifies sellers when another user purchases their listed photo card using points.
    * **Successful Sale Notification**

      * Notifies sellers when their listed photo card has been sold.
    * **Sold-Out Notification**

      * Notifies sellers when all copies of a listed photo card have been sold.
* Surprise Points

  * Provides users with one random-box draw opportunity every hour through a surprise modal.
  * The points obtained from the draw are added to the user's point balance.

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>
Choi Minkyung

</summary>
<div markdown="1">

* Photo Card Sales

  * **Sell**

    * Users can sell photo cards they currently own.
    * Users can select one of their owned photo cards, enter the quantity to sell within the total issuance limit, set the price per card, and list it on the marketplace.
  * **Edit**

    * Users can edit the sales information of photo cards they have listed.
* Photo Card Exchange

  * **Approve / Reject**

    * Sellers can approve or reject exchange proposals.
    * Sellers can view the list of exchange cards proposed by other users from the photo card detail page.
    * When a seller approves an exchange, ownership of the offered card and the listed photo card is exchanged between the two users.

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>
Kim Subin

</summary>
<div markdown="1">

* Success & Failure Modals
* My Photo Cards

  * Provides users with a personal gallery where they can view all photo cards they have purchased from the marketplace or registered as their own.
  * Includes filtering, sorting, search, and pagination features.
* My Listed Photo Cards

  * Provides a list of photo cards currently listed for sale by the user.
  * Includes filtering by grade, genre, sales method, and sold-out status, as well as pagination.

</div>
</details>

  * **Sign Up**

  * **Log In**

    * Users can access features according to the permissions associated with their account after logging in.
  * **Log Out**

    * Users can log out when they no longer want to remain signed in.

  * Users can sign up and log in using their Google account.
* Landing Page

  * Users who are not logged in are shown a landing page introducing the service.
  * Logged-in users are redirected to the main service instead of seeing the landing page.
